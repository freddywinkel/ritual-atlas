# Ritual Atlas image-generation prompt set

The deck artwork was created with built-in image generation for this personal
project. The approved Three of Wands image was used only as a palette and finish
reference, never as a composition or character template.

## Card prompt template

> Create an original, independent premium vertical tarot-oracle narrative for
> **[Tarot title] + [paired Oracle title]**. Show **[card-specific scene]** with
> expressive, anatomically convincing people and/or animals. Weave the two card
> ideas into one readable moment through gesture, environment, weather, light,
> and meaningful objects. Use cinematic depth, tactile painterly detail, midnight
> teal, indigo and mineral green with restrained champagne-gold light. Preserve a
> strong silhouette at thumbnail size and keep the main narrative inside the
> central 80 percent. Edge-to-edge portrait image. No printed title, number,
> border, logo, signature or watermark. Do not copy or reconstruct any existing
> deck artwork, signature composition, card back or guidebook illustration.

## Variation controls

Across the 79 images, vary age, ethnicity, gender presentation, body type,
mobility, family structure, camera distance, season, weather, architecture and
biome. Alternate intimate portraits, groups, overhead views, low angles, broad
landscapes and interior scenes. Use warmth, daylight and celebratory community
scenes as well as nocturnal or storm-lit images. Do not default to a recurring
dark-haired traveler, fox, path, doorway or lone glowing tree.

## Share-image prompt

> Create a premium midnight-teal social preview for Ritual Atlas. Use the
> approved original Three of Wands illustration as the right-side hero image,
> balanced with champagne-gold route lines and spacious editorial typography.
> Include the exact title “Ritual Atlas”, the English/Dutch personal-journal
> message, and no official deck branding or artist imitation.
